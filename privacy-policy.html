Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Joey Cycling LLC, doing business as CircuitCycling ("we," "us," or "our"), operates the CircuitCycling coaching service, including our automated coaching assistant, Armin AI. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information about our clients ("you").

Information We Collect

As part of enrolling in our coaching services, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Training and fitness data synced from TrainingPeaks (power output, heart rate, training load, and related metrics)
  • Messages and reports you send us via WhatsApp (training status, health updates, schedule changes)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide and improve our coaching services, including:

  • Planning and adjusting your personalized training program
  • Sending you training plan updates, workout reminders, and progress reports via WhatsApp and email
  • Responding to your questions and health/schedule reports

WhatsApp Messaging Consent

By enrolling as a CircuitCycling client and providing your phone number, you agree to receive coaching-related messages from Armin AI via WhatsApp, including training plans, reminders, and progress updates. Message frequency varies based on your service plan. Standard message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of WhatsApp messaging at any time by contacting us directly; please note that opting out of WhatsApp communication may affect our ability to deliver coaching services that depend on this channel.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share information with service providers necessary to operate our business (such as TrainingPeaks, our messaging provider, and our hosting provider), each bound to protect your data appropriately.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as you remain a client, and for a reasonable period afterward in case you return to our services or request historical records.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us using the information below.
